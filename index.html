EDIBLE · SUN LOVING

Sweet basil

A fragrant kitchen favorite for a sunny sill.

Give it 6+ hours of sun. Keep soil lightly moist and pinch off flower buds to encourage leafy growth.
🪴
LOW LIGHT · EASY

Pothos

A cheerful trailing plant that forgives a missed watering.

Happy in bright, indirect light or a shadier corner. Water when the top few centimeters of soil feel dry.
🍅
EDIBLE · SUN LOVING

Cherry tomato

Sweet little harvests for a sunny patio or balcony.

Choose a large pot, give it full sun and a sturdy support. Water consistently, especially in hot weather.
🌱
INDIRECT LIGHT · LEAFY

Boston fern

Soft, feathery foliage for a bright, humid corner.

Keep soil evenly moist, avoid harsh direct sun, and group with other plants to enjoy a little extra humidity.

A few helpful answers

New to this?
You're in good company.

Do I need a garden to get started?

Not at all. A windowsill pot, a balcony container, or even a jar of herbs can be a lovely place to begin. Match the plant to the light and space you have.

How often should I water my plants?

There isn't one schedule for every plant. Check the soil first: many houseplants prefer watering when the top layer feels dry. Pots, light, temperature, and season all make a difference.

Will my checklist stay saved?

Yes. Your tasks are saved in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data or switching devices may reset the list.

What grows well where I live?

Climate, season, and local conditions matter. Use a local extension service or a planting calendar for your area, and always check the plant’s needs before planting outdoors.
